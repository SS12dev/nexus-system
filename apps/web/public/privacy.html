Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-27

The short version

  • Nexus is a private AI assistant run by one person (the "owner") for themselves and a small number of invited people. It is not a commercial service and shows no ads.
  • We keep what you give it (chats, files, your setup answers) and what your assistant learns from it, so it can help you. You can see, edit, download and delete it.
  • Your messages are sent to an AI model provider (OpenAI) to generate replies. Data from your Google account is used only to do what you ask, and never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Other people on Nexus can't see your chats, files or memories. Usage records keep costs, not your words.

1. Who we are

Nexus at nexus.servhub.cc is operated by its owner, Shivam, who decides how your information is used (the "controller"). Contact: shivam12.dev@gmail.com. Access is by invitation only.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the assistant: answer your messages, search your files and memories, run the teams of AI agents you ask for, run your routines and notify you.
  • To keep Nexus working and secure: sign-in, rate limits, abuse prevention, backups and error logs.
  • To manage costs: each person has a monthly AI budget, and model usage is recorded with its cost.
  • To improve Nexus: every few days Nexus reviews how it is used and proposes improvements to the owner. The review uses counts (tools used, failures, costs), feedback people sent, and short anonymous topic labels of requests such as "trip planning". It never includes names, email addresses or chat text, and counts a topic only when several requests share it. You can leave your requests out in Settings › Help improve Nexus, or by turning memory off.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

4. Google user data

If you allow Google services when signing in, Nexus stores an encrypted refresh token and uses Google data only to do what you ask: for example, listing today's calendar events for a morning brief, finding an email you mention, saving a draft, or adding a task. Sending email, inviting people to events and deleting events always need your approval in the app first.

  • Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train or improve AI models, and not shared with anyone except as needed to answer you (the AI model provider in section 5 processes the parts needed for a reply).
  • People cannot read your Google data unless you ask your assistant to show it to them, except where required by law or for security.
  • Nexus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google at any time in Settings, which also revokes Nexus's access at Google, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Who can see your information

  • Other people on Nexus, including admins, cannot open your chats, files, memories or routines in the app.
  • The owner sees account details (name, email, role, budget, sign-in history), usage and cost per person, feedback you send, and errors. As the person running the computer Nexus is stored on, the owner could technically access the database; they will only do so to keep Nexus working or secure, or if required by law.
  • Apps that Nexus builds for you are public at their own web address by default, so anyone with the link can open them; you can make them private.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your chats, files, memories and routines stay until you delete them or your account.
  • Memories that no chat has confirmed for 60 days gradually lose confidence and are removed.
  • Sign-in sessions last 30 days. Logs and usage records are kept for operating and cost purposes, and backups are kept for a limited time before being overwritten.
  • When you delete your account, Nexus signs you out and keeps the account and its data for 30 days so you can request recovery. An owner or admin must approve recovery. After 30 days, permanent deletion is queued; failures removing managed resources may delay completion. Deleted data then disappears from backups as they rotate.
  • If you choose to start with a clean account, permanent deletion begins immediately. Nexus creates the new account only after the old account's data and managed resources have been removed.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit what your assistant remembers on the Memory page; turn learning off or forget everything.
  • Download your account, setup answers, chats, memories and routines in Settings › Download my data.
  • Delete chats, files and memories one by one, or your whole account in Settings › Delete my account. Account deletion signs you out immediately; recovery is available for 30 days only after an owner or admin approves it.
  • Turn off Google access, notifications, WhatsApp and the improvement review at any time in Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights (to object, restrict processing, or complain to a data protection authority). Email us and we'll help.

9. Security

Nexus is reached only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google tokens are encrypted at rest, secrets are kept encrypted, code your assistant runs is isolated from the internet and from your other data, and actions that send, publish or delete need your approval. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

10. Children

Nexus is for people aged 16 and over. Please don't invite or sign up anyone younger.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the version at the top changes and Nexus asks you to review it the next time you open the app.
