Jarvis privacy

Jarvis is a private assistant run by its owner for themselves and a small number of invited people. It is not a public service.

What Jarvis stores

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile picture, used to sign you in.
  • Your chats, the files you upload, and the memories and profile Jarvis builds from your chats. You can view, edit and delete memories and files in the app.
  • If you allow Google services (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Tasks) at sign-in, an encrypted refresh token so Jarvis's agents can act on them when you ask.

How Google data is used

Data from Google services is used only to carry out what you ask Jarvis to do, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. Message content may be sent to the AI model provider (OpenAI) to generate replies. Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

Each person's data is visible only to them. You can disconnect any Google service on the Connections page, which also revokes Jarvis's access at Google, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Ask the owner to delete your account and data.
