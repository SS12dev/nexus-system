# Creative briefs and concepts

## A brief (one page)

- **Background**: who the client is and why this project now.
- **Objective**: the one thing it must achieve, measurable if possible.
- **Audience**: who, what they think now, what we want them to think or do.
- **Single-minded message**: one sentence.
- **Tone**: three adjectives, and one thing it must not feel like.
- **Deliverables and formats**, sizes, channels.
- **Mandatories**: logo, legal lines, brand rules.
- **Timeline, budget, approvals.**

## Concept directions

Give 3-5 genuinely different directions, not variations. For each: a name, the idea in one
line, why it works for the audience, an example execution (headline, visual), and a risk.

## Moodboards

Describe the palette (with hex codes), type, imagery style, composition and references as
search terms or links found with `web_search`. Make images only if the `images` skill is
granted and the user asks.

## Critique

Lead with what works. Then judge against the brief: does it say the single-minded message to
that audience? Specific, actionable notes, most important first, with a suggested rewrite
for copy.
