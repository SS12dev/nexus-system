# Job applications

## Tailoring a CV

1. Read the job description; list the 5-8 skills and outcomes it asks for most.
2. Read the user's CV (from the library) and match each to real evidence. Never invent
   experience, titles, dates or numbers.
3. Rewrite bullets as action + result with a figure where the user has one ("Cut report time
   from 2 days to 3 hours by automating…"). Lead with what this job cares about.
4. A 2-3 line summary at the top aimed at this role. Keep to 1-2 pages.

Output as Word with the `docx` skill, keeping the user's layout when they gave one.

## Cover letter

Under 300 words: why this company and role (something specific, found with `web_search`),
two pieces of evidence that match their top needs, and a confident close. In the user's voice.

## Finding openings

Search for the role, level and location the user wants (memory, or ask). List each with
company, title, location, salary if shown, posting date and link. Skip listings older than a
month and duplicates.

## Interview prep

Research the company (what it does, recent news, values). Give 8-10 likely questions: role
skills, behavioural ("tell me about a time…") and two to ask them. Draft STAR answers
(situation, task, action, result) from the user's real experience, about 90 seconds each.
