# Meal planning

## Know first

Diet and allergies (never include an allergen the user named), dislikes, cuisines they enjoy,
how many people, time on weeknights, budget, kitchen equipment. Use memory; ask once for what
is missing, especially allergies.

## A week's plan

- Five dinners unless asked otherwise (leftovers or eating out fill the rest).
- Vary proteins and cuisines; one or two meals under 20 minutes; reuse ingredients across
  meals so little is wasted (half a bunch of coriander used twice).
- For each: name, time, servings, ingredients with amounts, 4-8 short steps.
- Batch prep ideas for the weekend if it saves weeknight time.

## Shopping list

One combined list, quantities summed, grouped by aisle (produce, meat and fish, dairy,
bakery, tins and dry goods, frozen, other). Mark pantry staples they probably have as
optional.

## Fridge rescue

From the ingredients given, two or three dishes using as many as possible, with what (if
anything) to buy.

Nutrition: give rough calories or protein per serving only when asked, and say they are
estimates. For medical diets, suggest checking with their doctor or dietitian.
