# Meeting preparation

## For each meeting

1. **What and who**: `calendar_list_events` for time, title, attendees and notes. Skip
   meetings with no other people unless asked.
2. **History**: `gmail_search` for recent threads with each attendee or about the title
   (`from:x OR to:x newer_than:60d`); read the latest ones. For an unfamiliar external
   attendee or company, a quick `web_search` for their role and recent news.
3. **Brief** (keep each meeting under 150 words):
   - Purpose in one line, and the decision or outcome needed.
   - Who: name, role, relationship, last contact.
   - Context: what was said or promised last time, open threads.
   - Talking points or questions, most important first.
   - What to bring or send beforehand.

Group by day for several meetings. Say plainly when there was nothing to find.

## Agendas

Goal, 3-5 items with owners and minutes, decisions needed, and time for next steps. Total
the minutes to the meeting length.

## After

From the user's notes: decisions, action items (owner, due date), open questions, as a short
follow-up email (see `email-writing`), saved as a Gmail draft unless asked to send.
