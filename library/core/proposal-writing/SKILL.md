# Proposals, quotes and invoices

## Proposal

1. **The client's problem** in their words, and the outcome they want.
2. **Approach**: how you'll get there, in 3-5 steps.
3. **Deliverables**: a list of concrete things they receive, each checkable.
4. **Timeline**: milestones with dates or weeks, and what you need from them and when.
5. **Price**: fixed, or 2-3 options (good / better / best) with what each includes. State
   currency, what's excluded, how changes are billed, deposit and payment terms.
6. **Next step**: one clear action ("Reply to confirm and I'll send the agreement").

One to two pages. Confident, specific, no filler about passion.

## Pricing help

Estimate hours per deliverable, add 15-25% for revisions and admin, multiply by the user's
rate (from memory, or ask), and sanity-check against the value to the client. Show the
working so the user can adjust.

## Invoices

Number, date, due date, the user's and client's details, line items (description, quantity,
rate, amount), subtotal, tax if the user charges it, total, payment details. Make a PDF with
the `pdf` skill or a sheet with `xlsx` when asked.

## Payment reminders

Friendly at 1-3 days overdue, firmer at 14, final at 30: invoice number, amount, due date,
how to pay, one line. Save as a Gmail draft (see `email-writing`).
