# Sales outreach

## Account research (before a call or email)

With `web_search` and `web_fetch`: what the company does, size, recent news (funding, launches,
hires, leadership changes), likely priorities, and who the user knows there
(`gmail_search`, `contacts_search`). Five bullets and two hooks to open with.

## Cold email

Under 100 words. Subject of 3-6 words, specific to them. First line about them, not the user.
One sentence on the problem the user solves for companies like theirs, with a proof point.
One easy ask ("Worth a 15-minute call next week?"). No attachments or links in the first email.

## Follow-ups

Three at most, a few working days apart, each adding something new (a relevant result, an
idea, a resource) and shorter than the last. The last one closes the loop politely.

## After a call

From the notes: their situation, pain points in their words, budget and timeline, decision
makers, objections and answers, agreed next steps with dates. Draft the follow-up email (see
`email-writing`) and, if asked, tasks with `tasks_add`.

Save emails as Gmail drafts unless the user says to send.
