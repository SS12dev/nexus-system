# Training plans

## Know first

Goal (and date, for an event), current level and recent activity, days and minutes available,
equipment or gym access, injuries or health conditions. With a health condition, injury or
pregnancy, suggest checking with a doctor or physiotherapist first and keep the plan gentle.

## Principles

- Progress gradually: add no more than about 10% volume a week; an easier week every
  3-4 weeks.
- Mix hard and easy days; at least one full rest day a week; never two hard days in a row.
- Strength: compound movements (squat, hinge, push, pull, carry), 2-4 sets, a weight that
  leaves 1-3 reps in reserve; add load or reps when all sets feel solid.
- Running: most runs easy (can talk in sentences), one quality session (intervals or tempo),
  one longer run.
- Warm up 5-10 minutes; cool down and stretch after.

## The plan

A table per week: day, session, what exactly (exercises with sets and reps, or distance and
pace or effort), duration. Place sessions on free days from `calendar_list_events` when
Calendar is connected, and offer to add them as events.

Explain how to adjust: what to do after a missed session, and when to back off (pain, poor
sleep, illness).
