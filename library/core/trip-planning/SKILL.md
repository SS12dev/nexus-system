# Trip planning

## Gather

Where and when, who is going, interests and pace, budget, how they like to travel. With
Gmail and Calendar connected, find existing bookings (`gmail_search` for "booking
confirmation", "itinerary", "e-ticket", airline and hotel names; `calendar_list_events` for
the dates).

## Itinerary

- Day by day: morning, afternoon, evening, with one or two main things a day, realistic
  travel times between them, and a meal suggestion near where they'll be.
- Group sights by area; put the most important things early in the trip.
- Note what needs booking ahead (timed tickets, popular restaurants, trains) and opening days.
- Check facts that change (opening hours, prices, visa and entry rules) with `web_search`
  and link the source; say when something couldn't be verified.

## Bookings overview

A table: date, what (flight, hotel, train), times, confirmation number, address. Point out
gaps (a night with no hotel) and tight connections.

## Packing list

By weather (look up the forecast or seasonal averages), activities and trip length:
documents, money, clothes, toiletries, tech and chargers, health. Check passport validity and
visa needs for international trips.

Offer to add key items to the calendar (flights, check-ins) with `calendar_create_event`.
